# Business Requirements

| ID | Name | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| BR-001 | Integrated Core ERP Operations | The business shall manage Sales, Purchase, Inventory, CRM, and Accounting/Finance through an integrated ERP solution. |
| BR-002 | Sales Management | The business shall support the sales lifecycle from quotation request through delivery, invoicing, and payment recording. |
| BR-003 | Purchase Management | The business shall support purchasing from RFQ creation through goods receipt, invoice review, and supplier payment. |
| BR-004 | Goods Receipt | The business shall control inbound goods receipt, quantity/quality checking, receipt validation, and receipt documentation. |
| BR-005 | Goods Issue | The business shall control outbound stock through delivery-order review, stock checking, issue validation, and shipment documentation. |
| BR-006 | Stock Count and Adjustment | The business shall support stock-count requests, counting, variance investigation/approval, recount, and stock adjustment. |
| BR-007 | Internal Stock Transfer | The business shall support transfer of stock between warehouse locations based on source-stock availability and receiving inspection. |
| BR-008 | CRM and Customer Care | The business shall support customer contact, opportunity management, quotation/order handling, and post-sale feedback. |
| BR-009 | Customer Receivables | The business shall support customer invoicing, payment confirmation, and periodic receivables updates. |
| BR-010 | Overdue Receivables | The business shall support monitoring and follow-up of overdue customer invoices. |
| BR-011 | Supplier Payables | The business shall support reconciliation, posting, payment, confirmation, and periodic summarization of supplier debt. |
| BR-012 | Customer Refund | The business shall support validation and processing of customer refunds using credit notes linked to original invoices. |
| BR-013 | Supplier Refund | The business shall support refund requests to suppliers and record supplier refunds using linked credit notes. |
| BR-014 | Master Data | The business shall maintain core data used by processes, including customers, products, suppliers, warehouses/locations, invoices, and related transaction records. |
| BR-015 | Integrated Information for Decisions | ERP data from different modules shall be integrated to support operational monitoring and business decision-making. |
| BR-016 | Online/Social Integration – Scope Statement | The source report states an intent to connect/synchronize the retail system with social-media platforms such as Facebook and TikTok; no detailed functional flow is specified. |
