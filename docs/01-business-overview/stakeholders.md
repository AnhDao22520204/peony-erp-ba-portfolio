# Stakeholders and Business Actors

| Actor / Stakeholder | Business involvement |
|---|---|
| Customer | Receives quotations, confirms purchase, pays, provides feedback, may request refund |
| Sales Staff | Receives customer requests, creates/sends quotations, creates orders/invoices, confirms customer payment, follows up customers |
| Purchasing Staff | Creates RFQs/purchase orders, communicates with suppliers, reviews supplier invoices |
| Warehouse Staff / Warehouse Keeper | Receives, checks, stores, issues, counts, and transfers goods |
| Warehouse Manager / Manager | Reviews stock/count activities and participates in stock issue/transfer decisions |
| Accountant | Invoicing, payment recording, receivables/payables, overdue follow-up, refunds |
| Supplier | Responds to RFQs, supplies goods, provides invoices, handles supplier-side refund requests |
| Requesting Department | Requests stock count and receives count results |
| Delivery Staff / Delivery Party | Participates in receipt confirmation/sign-off where documented |
