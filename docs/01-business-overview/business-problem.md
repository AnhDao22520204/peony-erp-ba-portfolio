# Business Problem

The source report describes a retail context with diverse products, many transactions, multiple suppliers, and the need to manage sales, purchasing, inventory, accounting/finance, and customers consistently. Managing these activities independently or manually makes it difficult to maintain a unified view of operations.

The ERP project therefore focuses on integrating these activities in one system so that business data can be shared across modules and operational processes can be standardized and tracked.

## Business needs evidenced in the source

- Manage sales transactions and revenue.
- Manage purchasing from suppliers, orders, and costs.
- Control inventory to reduce shortages or excess stock.
- Manage accounting transactions, receivables/payables, and payment history.
- Manage customer information and customer-care activities.
- Integrate data across modules to support management decisions.
