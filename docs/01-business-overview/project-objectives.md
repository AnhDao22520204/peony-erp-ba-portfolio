# Project Objectives

1. Apply ERP to manage the core Peony operations: Sales, Purchase, Inventory, CRM, and Accounting/Finance.
2. Integrate data between ERP modules to support accurate and timely business decisions.
3. Improve tracking of sales, purchases, inventory, and financial/accounting activities.
4. Reduce manual work and operational errors through integrated workflows.
5. Support customer management and customer experience through CRM-related processes.
6. Understand and manage interactions among ERP modules, including sales, stock movements, and finance.
7. Provide a basis for further expansion of ERP functions and integrations.
