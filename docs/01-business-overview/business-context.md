# Business Context

Peony is presented as a fashion retailer selling clothing and accessories. Its operating model is primarily B2C: products are purchased from partner companies/suppliers and then sold to customers. The report states that operation is handled manually in combination with Odoo and that Odoo deployment was planned/used in May 2025.

The business needs to coordinate information concerning products, suppliers, customers, sales, purchasing, inventory, accounting/finance, and customer relationships on an integrated ERP platform.


## Business model elements documented in the report

- Customer segment: primarily young consumers; report emphasizes ages 18–30.
- Channels: e-commerce marketplaces, website, social media, and word of mouth.
- Customer relationship: consultation through email, Facebook, website, and e-commerce channels.
- Key activities: selling/managing products, packaging/fulfillment, marketing, customer care.
- Key partners: suppliers, garment-related partners, and designers.
